Using Bookmark'r

  • New link: click “Add link,” enter a title and URL, then choose emoji and color presets or type your own Unicode and hex codes. Check the previews, then Save.
  • Edit: tap the ⠿ handle on a tile, or long-press the tile background, to open it for editing or deletion.
  • Reorder: drag a tile’s ⠿ handle with a mouse or finger; the order is saved. With a keyboard, focus the handle and use the arrow keys. Drag near the top or bottom of the screen to scroll.
  • Line breaks: click “Add break” to insert a row separator; drag anywhere on the strip to move it, or long-press to remove it. You can also focus the strip and use the arrow keys.
  • Open links: click the icon on a tile to open in a new tab.
  • Reset: clears the board and deletes Bookmark'r’s saved data in this browser after confirmation.
